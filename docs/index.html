How this works

  • Each month, the share of objects you read moves back to Standard. Everything else gets 30 days older. Autoclass moves objects to Nearline after 30 days without access. With an Archive terminal class, they move on to Coldline at 90 days and Archive at 365.
  • Autoclass adds a management fee of per 1,000 objects per month. Objects under 128 KiB are never moved and aren't counted.
  • Enabling Autoclass on an existing bucket costs one Class A operation per object. Reading an object back from Coldline or Archive costs one Class A operation. Retrieval and early-deletion fees don't apply with Autoclass.
  • Network transfer and your own read and write operations are left out, since they cost the same either way.
  • Prices are Google Cloud list prices in USD, last changed , and are checked weekly. Your actual bill may differ.
